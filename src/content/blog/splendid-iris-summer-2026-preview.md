## Why Splendid Iris Works for Texas Homes

A few reasons we keep ordering from them:

### 1. The aesthetic ages well
Their pieces don't read as a "2024 trend" — they sit in the same lane as old French country with a slightly modernized hand. A Splendid Iris piece you buy in 2026 will not look dated in 2031.

### 2. The price points
Most decor pieces in our cases run $25–$80. There are statement pieces above that, but the meat of their line is in the "you can buy this and it doesn't feel reckless" zone. That's our sweet spot.

### 3. They photograph well
For Texans who entertain — and that's a lot of you — pieces that look good on the table at a Sunday brunch and *also* look good in the photos your friend is texting around afterward matter. Splendid Iris consistently delivers on both.
