## Why Ty Specifically

Two honest reasons.

**Price-to-perceived-value.** A $6.99 plush reads as a real gift to a child in a way that a $6.99 anything else does not. For a party favor or a stocking, that ratio is hard to beat.

**Collectibility without commitment.** Kids who collect them are genuinely happy to get any of them, which removes the guessing. You do not need to know a six-year-old's taste to succeed here.

## A Note on Timing

The Ty wall is at its fullest in **early November** and at its thinnest the week before Christmas. That isn't a sales pitch — it's just how a small shop's reorder cycle works against a national supply of plush animals in December.

If you buy stocking stuffers in November, you get selection. If you buy them on December 20th, you get what's left, and so does everyone else in Kaufman County.
