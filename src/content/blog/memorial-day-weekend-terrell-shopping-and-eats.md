## A Long Weekend, an Easy Plan

Memorial Day 2026 is **Monday, May 25**, which means a three-day weekend stretching Saturday May 23 through Monday May 25. If you're avoiding the lake-traffic mass exodus and looking for a low-key way to spend a few hours close to home, **The Shops at Terrell** (formerly Tanger Outlets Terrell) is one of the easiest half-day plans in Kaufman County.

Here's our suggested loop, including where we fit in.

## The Half-Day Plan

**10:30 AM — Arrive.** Free parking, biggest lot near the Nike anchor. Saturday morning before noon is by far the calmest window of the weekend.

**10:45 AM — Outlet anchors.** Hit two or three:
- **Levi's** for any denim refresh before summer
- **Old Navy Outlet** for kids' summer basics
- **Famous Footwear** for sandals or kids' growing-foot replacements
- **Crocs** if you have any kids in your life under 12

Don't try to hit everything. Pick two, get out.

**11:30 AM — Snack stop.** **Rocky Mountain Chocolate** for a small treat. Or, depending on what's currently rotating in the food court at the center, grab a quick lunch.

**12:00 PM — Gugu Gifts (Suite 112).** This is our pitch: 15 minutes max. If you have a Memorial Day cookout this weekend, walk out with a hostess gift. If summer birthdays are coming up, get ahead of them. If you just want a candle, we have that too.

**12:30 PM — Optional add-on.** **Hometown Cinema** at the center is showing the big summer releases — turn the half-day into a full one if the kids are up for it.

**1:30 PM — Home** with two outlet bags, a wrapped gift, and a movie behind you.

## What to Bring to a Memorial Day Cookout

Honest answer from us: don't bring more food. Every host has more food than they need. What they don't have:

- **Greenleaf Citron Sol candle ($12)** — bright, lemony, sets the outdoor patio mood without competing with grill smoke
- **Crow Canyon Enamelware platter ($36–$40)** — they unwrap it, immediately use it for the watermelon, you're a legend
- **Voluspa Lychee 5.5 oz ($22)** — for the host who already lives this aesthetic
- A small **Glory Haus mug ($20)** — for the host's morning-after coffee, with a card

Anything in the $20–$45 range hits the right note: thoughtful, not extravagant, doesn't make the host's gift back to you next year a competition.

## Practical Notes for Memorial Day

- **Hours**: most stores at the center keep regular Saturday hours. Monday Memorial Day has slightly reduced hours. Sunday is typical.
- **Parking**: free and plentiful. Saturday after 1 PM gets busier; arrive earlier if you can.
- **The drive**: I-20 Exit 501. Coming from Heartland, Travis Ranch, Forney, or anywhere in Kaufman County, you're 10–20 minutes out.
- **Restaurants in Terrell proper**: if you want to extend the day, Historic Downtown Terrell has lunch options worth a 5-minute drive.
