## What If He Already Has Everything?

We hear this a lot for older dads, granddads, or fathers-in-law who insist they "don't need anything." Two moves that work for those guys:

1. **A nicer version of something he already uses every day.** A Kaweco pen if he scribbles notes. A real Crow Canyon enamelware mug if his current one is chipped. Govino glasses if he's been drinking out of plastic for six years.
2. **A small ribbon-wrapped package, not a big box.** Older dads especially read "fanfare" as wasteful. A small thoughtful gift wins.
