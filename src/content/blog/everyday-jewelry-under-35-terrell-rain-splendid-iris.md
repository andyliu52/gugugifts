## The Under-$40 Gift Strategy

This price band solves a specific problem — the gift that has to feel personal without being a commitment. Teacher, coworker, your kid's friend's mom, the person who hosted last weekend.

- **One pair, wrapped: $13–$25.** Reads as thoughtful, not heavy.
- **Two pairs for two people: under $40 total.** The move when you have several to cover.
- **Earrings plus a card:** still under $30, and the card does more work than people expect.

Everything gets wrapped free at the counter, so you can walk in without a gift bag in the car.
