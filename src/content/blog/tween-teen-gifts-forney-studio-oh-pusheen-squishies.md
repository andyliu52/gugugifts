## The Hardest List

Little kids are easy and adults are manageable. The 9-to-15 range is where holiday lists go to die — too old for toys, too young for gift cards to feel like anything.

What actually works for this age is **small, cute, and theirs**: something for the backpack, the desk, or the bathroom counter that nobody else in the house is going to borrow. That's most of one wall at Gugu Gifts.

From **Forney**, we're about **15 minutes east on I-20** — Exit 501, The Shops at Terrell.
