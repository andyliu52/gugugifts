## A Box Is Easier Than a Gift

Every box in this post was put together from what's on our shelves in Suite 112 — no stock photos, nothing ordered in. We built them to answer the question we hear most from October on: *what do I get someone when I don't know exactly what they want?*

The answer, most of the time, is three or four smaller things that go together. A box takes the pressure off any single item being perfect. It also looks like a lot more effort than it was.

Prices below are rough — the total moves a few dollars depending on scent, color, and what's in stock that week.

![Mary Square to-do notepad, Greenleaf Gingerbread sachet and pen in a kraft gift box, Gugu Gifts, Terrell TX](/images/products/gift-boxes/mary-square-greenleaf-desk-box.jpg)

A Mary Square to-do notepad, a matching pen, and a Greenleaf Gingerbread sachet tucked in so the whole thing smells like December when it's opened.

**Who it's for:** the coworker, the teacher, the neighbor who takes in your packages. It's the box version of "I thought about you," and it lands under twenty dollars.

## How to Get One

Bring a budget and a name. Tell us who it's for, and we'll help you pull the pieces together from the shelves — it usually takes about ten minutes. Everything is **wrapped free at the counter**.

![Kraft gift box tied with burgundy velvet ribbon, Gugu Gifts, Terrell TX](/images/products/gift-boxes/wrapped-box-velvet-ribbon.jpg)

**Come in before mid-November if you can.** The boxes above are built from current stock, and the pieces that make them work — the Gorky shakers, the Beekman sets, the hydrangea Swig — come in small quantities. By December, we're building around what's left.
