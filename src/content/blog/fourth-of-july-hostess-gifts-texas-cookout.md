## July 4th in Texas Is Its Own Thing

Independence Day 2026 falls on a **Saturday**, which means cookouts will run all weekend — Friday July 3rd evenings, Saturday all day, Sunday-morning leftovers. If you're attending a cookout in Forney, Heartland, Travis Ranch, Devonshire, Gateway Parks, Heath, Rockwall, Mesquite, or anywhere in Kaufman County, the question is: *what do you bring?*

The lazy answer is a six-pack and a bag of ice. The thoughtful answer involves slightly more thought and slightly less alcohol. Here's what we'd suggest.

## What Hosts Actually Want

We've polled Texas hosts. The pattern:

- They have **plenty of food**. Don't bring more.
- They have **plenty of beer**. Adding one more cooler of it doesn't move the needle.
- They have **not enough small thoughtful gifts** that survive past the cookout.

A $20–$40 thoughtful gift outranks a $20 case of beer in their memory by Sunday morning. Here's what we'd put in a cookout-bag.

## What Specifically Doesn't Work

- Anything with literal stars-and-stripes that they'll only use one weekend a year
- Specialty dips or food items unless you know their dietary preferences
- Wine to a beer crowd, beer to a wine crowd
- "Patriotic" candles — fragrance over season usually misses
