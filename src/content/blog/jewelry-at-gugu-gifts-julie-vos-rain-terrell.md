## The Corner People Linger In

Jewelry is the section of **Gugu Gifts** where people slow down. There's a mirror, a gold display case, and a full slatwall of earrings and necklaces — and almost everyone who walks in ends up holding something against their collarbone to see how it sits.

Here's what we run, and how to shop it as a gift.

## Julie Vos — the Heirloom-at-a-Reachable-Price Line

**Julie Vos** anchors the locked case. It's the 24K-gold-plate brand you keep seeing on wrists at weddings and brunches across Kaufman and Rockwall counties — textured finish, little chain bracelets, resin drop earrings in event-matching colors. It wears well over years without crossing into fine-jewelry pricing, which makes it the go-to for milestone gifts: graduations, first jobs, "you became a mom this year." (We wrote a whole post on [why Texans choose Julie Vos](/blog/julie-vos-jewelry-why-texans-choose-it) if you want the deep dive.)

## Rain — the Playful, Colorful Spinner

The **Rain** tower is the opposite energy, in the best way — artisan earrings with turquoise, abalone, hand-painted enamel, little Texas and Western motifs. This is where you go for the friend whose style is *fun*, or when you want a striking piece that doesn't cost much. It's also our most-spun display; there's always something new on it.

## Everyday Gold & the Hoops

For the person who wears the same two pieces every day, we keep a run of everyday gold — hypoallergenic hoops and studs from lines like **Brenda Grands** and **Beekman 1802**, the kind of pieces that go on and don't come off. Safe, classic, and easy to gift because almost everyone wears gold.

## The Bracelet Bar

The bracelet trays are made for stacking — beaded, chain, cuff, and the little word bracelets ("Blessed," initials, milestone numbers). Pick two or three that look good together and you've built a custom stack for under what one designer bangle would cost.

## How to Pick a Gift Piece

- **Don't know their taste?** Everyday gold hoops or studs are nearly foolproof.
- **Want it to feel special?** A Julie Vos piece, wrapped, with a card.
- **Shopping for a personality?** The Rain spinner — match the piece to the person.
- **Pair it up:** any earring + a Voluspa candle = a complete, generous-feeling gift around $40.

## See It in Person

Online photos flatter; in person you feel the weight and see the real finish. Come hold a few pieces at the case.
