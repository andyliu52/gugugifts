## Meet Us at 301 Tanger Drive

If you've driven I-20 east of Dallas, you've seen the signs: **The Shops at Terrell** — the outlet center most locals still call **Tanger Outlets Terrell**. The center kept the address (301 Tanger Dr, Terrell, TX 75160) and most of its anchor stores when ownership changed. What's new on the property is us: **Gugu Gifts in Suite 112**, a curated mid-luxe gift shop right inside the same complex you're already planning to visit.

Whether you're driving in from Forney, Rockwall, Mesquite, Greenville, Canton, or coming from Dallas proper, here's a sensible game plan for an outlet-day stop that doesn't end with you forgetting a birthday gift on the way home.

## Who's at The Shops at Terrell Right Now

The big anchors are still pulling traffic off I-20:

- **Nike** — the perennial reason half the cars in the lot are here
- **Levi's** — denim outlet pricing on jeans and jackets
- **Gap Outlet** and **Old Navy Outlet** — back-to-school, basics, and rotating clearance racks
- **Reebok** and **Crocs** — sneakers and slip-ons at outlet markdowns
- **Famous Footwear** — wide brand selection under one roof
- **Rocky Mountain Chocolate** — the impulse-buy stop on your way back to the car

Beyond the anchors, the directory rotates with names like **Sunglass Hut**, **Skechers**, **Adidas**, **Jockey**, **Claire's**, **Torrid**, **Lane Bryant**, **Maurices**, **Carter's**, **Hanesbrands**, **L'eggs Hanes Bali Playtex**, **Perfumania**, and **Hometown Cinema** for when the family wants a movie after shopping.

For the live, official directory, the management office posts updates at [theshopsatterrell.com](https://www.theshopsatterrell.com/) — call ahead at **(972) 524-6034** if there's a specific store you're driving in for.

## Practical Tips for the Drive

- **Exit**: Take **I-20 Exit 501** (FM 148/Tanger Drive) and follow signs into the center. Suite 112 is on the main shopping loop — look for the gold lettering.
- **Parking**: Free, plentiful. Saturdays before noon are noticeably calmer than mid-afternoon.
- **Around 75160**: While you're in Terrell, **Ben E. Keith Park**, the **Historic Downtown Square**, and the **Terrell Heritage Museum** are short drives if you want to make a half-day of it.
- **Construction note**: I-20 widening and TxDOT projects between Forney and Terrell can add time on weekday afternoons — give yourself a 15-minute buffer if you're coming westbound on Friday between 4–6 PM.
