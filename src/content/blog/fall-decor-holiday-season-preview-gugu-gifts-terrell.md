## Fall in Texas Arrives on the Calendar Before It Arrives Outside

It's October in Kaufman County, which means it's still 88 degrees and everyone has decided it's autumn anyway. Fair enough. The decor goes up, the scents change, and the first real question of the season shows up: *who's hosting Thanksgiving?*

This is the month the shop turns over completely. Here's what's on the shelves, and the honest case for starting your holiday shopping now instead of in December.

## The Fall Scent Shift

The single biggest change in the store this month is fragrance. Summer's citrus and coconut are out; warmer, heavier scents are in.

- **Voluspa** — the fall range is where the brand is at its best. Warm, layered, and the jars themselves are decorative enough that people leave them out year-round. If you've only ever bought the bright summer scents, the fall ones are a different experience.
- **Greenleaf** — the **$3 sachets** remain the smartest thing in the store. Buy three fall scents, put them in a drawer or a car, and figure out what you actually like before spending $34 on a diffuser.
- **Greenleaf Flower Diffusers ($34)** — no flame, months of throw, and the right answer for anyone with pets, small kids, or a rental with a no-candles clause.
- **Birch & Bloom Tin Candle ($15)** — lidded and portable, our standard recommendation for a hostess gift you hand over at the door.

If you're not sure where to start, our [Voluspa scent guide](/blog/voluspa-scent-guide-choosing-the-right-candle) breaks down how to choose one as a gift rather than for yourself — different problem, different answer.

## Thanksgiving Tablescaping Starts Now, Not in November

Here's the thing people learn the hard way: **serving pieces don't get restocked in late November.** What's on the shelf in October is largely what there is.

If you're hosting this year:

- **Pampa Bay** — the pieces that make a table look considered without looking fussy. Our [tablescaping guide](/blog/tablescaping-pampa-bay-splatterware-terrell) covers how the splatterware and serving pieces work together.
- **Crow Canyon Enamelware ($14–$40)** — durable, photographs well, and survives a table with kids at it. The **oval serving platter ($36–$40)** is the piece that gets used every year.
- **Govino Glass Set ($30)** — unbreakable stemware for a table where someone will absolutely knock a glass over.

The pattern we see: people come in the second week of November looking for one specific platter, and it went in early October. Come look now, even if you're not buying yet.

## The Case for Starting Holiday Shopping in October

We understand this sounds like a shop telling you to shop. Here's the actual reasoning:

**1. The good pieces are one-offs.** A lot of what we carry — particularly **Julie Vos** and **Rain** jewelry — comes in limited quantities. When a piece sells, it's often gone for the season. December shopping means picking from what's left.

**2. You buy better when you're not panicking.** A gift chosen in October for a specific person beats a gift chosen on December 20th for whoever's left on the list. This is the entire difference between a gift someone keeps and a gift someone politely thanks you for.

**3. You can spread the cost.** Six gifts at $30 across October and November is a manageable line item. Six gifts on December 18th is a bad weekend.

**4. December in here is genuinely busy.** The unhurried version of this shop — where we can walk you through scents, talk through who you're buying for, and wrap everything properly — is the October and November version.

## Fall Gifting Occasions People Forget

It's not just Thanksgiving and Christmas. Between now and the end of November:

- **Homecoming and mum season** — small jewelry gifts get bought here more than you'd think
- **Fall birthdays** — October and November are dense with them
- **Teacher conferences** — a $3 sachet and a card at conference time is a small, well-timed gesture
- **Friendsgiving** — usually the weekend *before* Thanksgiving, and hosts get overlooked because it's "casual." A **$14 enamelware plate** fixes that.
- **New neighbors** — fall is a heavy moving season in the Forney and Heartland new builds. Our [housewarming guide](/blog/housewarming-gifts-heartland-forney-new-builds) covers that specifically.
