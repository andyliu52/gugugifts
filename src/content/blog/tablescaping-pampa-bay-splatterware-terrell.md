## For the One Who Hosts

Every friend group has a host — the person whose table always looks like a magazine, who somehow has the right bowl for everything. Shopping for them is easy once you know the entertaining shelves at **Gugu Gifts**. Here's what to reach for.

## Pampa Bay — the Gold-and-White Showpieces

If you've admired the gold-rimmed bowls and the gleaming porcelain pieces on our front counter, that's **Pampa Bay**. The textured gold bowls, the organic-edge white dishes trimmed in gold, the little nesting bowls — they photograph beautifully and they're sturdier than they look. One statement bowl is a gift on its own; it's the thing that goes out on the table and gets the "where is this from?"

## Splatterware — the Everyday Color

On the open shelves we run **splatterware** dinnerware and enamelware — the speckled bowls, plates, platters, and mugs in blue, red, and confetti patterns. It's the casual, indestructible, charming side of the table — perfect for a cookout, a cabin, a first apartment, or anyone who wants color that doesn't chip its way out of the rotation.

## Govino & the Glassware

For drinks, **Govino** shatterproof glassware is our quiet hero — wine, flutes, and tumblers that look like glass but bounce. Pool decks, boat days, and outdoor tables in Texas summers were made for these. We also carry wine chillers and decanters for the more serious setup.

## Bundt & the Bakers

The gold **Nordic Ware Bundt** pans — the anniversary pan, the Bundtlette set — are a sleeper gift for the baker. They make a beautiful cake and they look good enough to hang. Pair one with a sweet little towel or a cake mix and you've got a complete "bake something" gift.
