## A Realistic Loop With Kids in Tow

If you're a parent in Forney, Heartland, Travis Ranch, Devonshire, Gateway Parks, Mesquite, Rockwall, or anywhere in Kaufman County, you've probably already learned: a "quick stop" at any retail center with kids under 10 is rarely actually quick.

Here's a realistic loop for **The Shops at Terrell** (formerly Tanger Outlets) with kids — built for survival, not Instagram.

## The 90-Minute Family Loop

### 0–10 min: Arrive and reset
Free parking, plentiful. Park near the **Old Navy / Levi's** anchor cluster — biggest cluster of kid-friendly stores within a short walking radius. Use the bathroom *before* shopping starts. Always.

### 10–35 min: Old Navy Outlet
This is the family anchor. Kid uniforms, summer basics, dresses for upcoming weddings, swim. Start here while everyone has fresh patience.

### 35–45 min: Crocs or Famous Footwear
Kids' feet grow at terrifying speed. Crocs is faster (less trying-on); Famous Footwear has more selection but takes longer. Pick one based on patience reserves.

### 45–55 min: SNACK STOP
Critical timing. Around this point, kid patience drops sharply. **Rocky Mountain Chocolate** is a beloved detour for an older kid; the rotating food trucks at the center are great for hungry-not-meltdown energy. Negotiate a reward, set expectations for the rest of the loop.

### 55–70 min: Gugu Gifts (Suite 112)
This is where parents are sometimes surprised: **bringing kids into a curated mid-luxe gift shop is fine**. Our shop is family-friendly. Browsing parents while kids look at the Meri Meri kids' display, the bamboo baby section ($12–$30 items), or the stuffed-toy corner is normal. We've watched countless families do this loop.

If you need a gift while you're here — Mother's Day, birthday, teacher gift, "we're going to dinner tonight" — knock it out in 10 minutes. Walk out wrapped.

### 70–90 min: Either home or extend
- **Home**: One last bathroom stop, back in the car. Two outlet bags, one wrapped gift, kids satisfied.
- **Extend**: **Hometown Cinema** is at the same complex. If a movie's playing they'd like and patience holds, you've got a full afternoon plan.

## The "Come With Just One Kid" Bonus

If you bring just one child (especially a 6-12 year old who can hold attention longer), the loop expands:

- **Old Navy + a small toy or accessory pickup at Gugu**
- **A snack negotiation about chocolate**
- **A 15-minute Gugu Gifts visit** where they get to "help" pick out a gift for grandma or aunt — kids genuinely love being told they have grown-up taste

Kids who are involved in the gift selection feel ownership and brag about it later. We've watched this play out dozens of times.

## Practical Parent Tips for The Shops at Terrell

- **Bathrooms** are at the center hub
- **Parking** never an issue, even Saturdays — pick anywhere
- **Strollers welcome** in all the stores we've shopped
- **Kid-tolerance time** at this complex is roughly 90 minutes for kids under 6, 2 hours for 6+. Plan accordingly.
