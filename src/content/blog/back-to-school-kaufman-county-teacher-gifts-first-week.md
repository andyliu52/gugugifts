## The Two Weeks That Sneak Up on Everyone

Mid-August in Kaufman County means the same scramble every year: supply lists you swore you'd handle in July, a meet-the-teacher night you found out about three days prior, and a lunchbox situation that needs solving by Monday.

If you're in Forney, Heartland, Travis Ranch, Devonshire, Gateway Parks, Terrell, Mesquite, or Rockwall, we're a short drive off **I-20 Exit 501** — and most of the back-to-school gaps people come in with take about fifteen minutes to close.

Here's what actually gets bought this time of year, and what we'd steer you toward.

## Meet-the-Teacher Night: What to Bring (and What Not To)

The honest read from teachers we've talked to: they are handed a lot of mugs. They are handed a lot of apple-themed things. What they're short on is something that makes the first week *easier*.

### Under $25

- **Greenleaf Sachet ($3)** — small, thoughtful, goes in a desk drawer or a classroom closet. Pair it with a card and you have a genuinely nice $6 gesture.
- **Birch & Bloom Tin Candle ($15)** — for the teacher's home, not the classroom. Lidded, travels well in a tote.
- **Kaweco Sport pen ($25)** — the gift for the teacher who writes constantly and has never owned a good pen. This one gets remembered.
- **Glory Haus mug ($20)** — yes, a mug. But a nice one, and if you know they've been using a chipped freebie for three years, it lands.

### Under $40

- **Greenleaf Citron Sol Diffuser ($34)** — bright citrus, no flame, safe for a classroom or a teacher's lounge shelf. This is our most-recommended teacher gift for a reason.
- **Crow Canyon Enamelware Marbled Plate ($14)** plus a small candle — two items, one ribbon, reads like more than it cost.

### The gift-card-in-a-nice-package move

If you genuinely don't know the teacher yet — very common at meet-the-teacher — a small item plus a card beats guessing. A **$3 sachet** tucked into a wrapped card is a better first impression than a random $30 object they didn't want.

## Lunchboxes, Water Bottles, and the Swig Situation

**Swig drinkware** is the back-to-school category that moves fastest for us, and it's mostly parents solving the same two problems.

- **The bottle that survives a backpack.** Swig's insulated bottles hold ice through a Texas school day, which matters in August when campuses are still running 100-degree afternoons.
- **The cup the parent keeps for themselves.** A significant share of "school" Swig purchases go to the adult in the car line. We don't judge.

If you have a kid starting middle school, the water bottle is a social object as much as a functional one. Worth letting them pick.

## The Stationery Corner Does Real Work in August

Our [stationery corner](/blog/stationery-corner-gugu-gifts-terrell) earns its keep this month:

- **Planners** for the high schooler who is about to have five teachers and no system
- **Cards** for the "thank you for a great first week" note that teachers genuinely keep
- **Kaweco Sport pens ($25)** for the college kid, the AP student, or the parent tracking four schedules

Paper planners are having a moment with students again — a phone calendar competes with every notification on the device, and a planner doesn't.

## The College Drop-Off Run

If you've got a kid heading to A&M-Commerce, UNT, UT Tyler, or anywhere further, the dorm gift categories that hold up:

- **Birch & Bloom Tin Candle ($15)** — lidded, dorm-safe storage, doesn't leak in a bin
- **Greenleaf Sachets ($3)** — for a dorm closet or laundry bag, and cheap enough to send four
- **Govino tumbler ($15)** — unbreakable, dishwasher-safe, survives a shared kitchen
- **Pearl Drop Earrings ($22)** or **Chloe earrings ($24)** — the small "I'm proud of you" gift that fits in a card envelope

The move we see most: a parent buys three or four small things, we wrap them separately, and they get handed out over move-in weekend instead of all at once.

## Dates Worth Knowing

Most Kaufman County districts start classes in **mid-August**, with meet-the-teacher nights falling in the week prior. Check your specific campus — Forney ISD, Terrell ISD, Crandall ISD, and Kaufman ISD don't always line up.

Our advice: handle teacher gifts the week *before* meet-the-teacher, not the night of. The night-of crowd is real, and the good stuff goes.
