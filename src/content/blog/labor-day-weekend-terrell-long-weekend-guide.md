## The Last Long Weekend Before Everything Gets Busy

**Labor Day 2026 is Monday, September 7**, giving you Saturday the 5th through Monday the 7th. In Kaufman County it's the unofficial hinge of the year: school's been in for three weeks, football has started, and it's the last three-day stretch before the fall calendar fills up with homecoming, fall break, and the holiday run.

It's also still going to be hot. Plan accordingly — this is an indoor-leaning weekend.

## The Low-Effort Saturday Plan

**10:00 AM — Arrive at The Shops at Terrell.** Free parking, and Saturday morning before noon is comfortably the calmest window of the three days.

**10:15 AM — Two anchors, not six.** Labor Day weekend is one of the better sale windows of the year at the outlets:
- **Levi's** — end-of-summer denim markdowns before the fall inventory lands
- **Old Navy Outlet** — the kids have already outgrown what you bought in August
- **Famous Footwear** — cleats, school shoes, or both

Pick two. Trying to do the whole center with a family is how a pleasant morning becomes a forced march.

**11:15 AM — Snack.** **Rocky Mountain Chocolate**, or whatever food trucks are rotating through the center that weekend.

**11:45 AM — Gugu Gifts (Suite 112).** Fifteen minutes. If you've got a cookout Sunday or Monday, walk out with the host gift handled and wrapped.

**12:30 PM — Optional.** **Hometown Cinema** is at the same complex, and the September releases are usually a decent way to spend the hottest part of the afternoon.
