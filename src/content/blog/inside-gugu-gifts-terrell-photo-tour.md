## Come On In

Most people find us by accident — they're walking the Tanger Outlets in Terrell, they see the wordmark, and they wander in "just to look." Twenty minutes later they're holding a candle, a card, and a little something for a friend they suddenly remembered. That's the shop working exactly as intended.

If you haven't made it in yet, here's a walk-through of what's waiting at **301 Tanger Dr, Suite 112**.

## The Front Counter

The first thing you see is the counter tablescape — a rotating mix of the prettiest things we have in right now. Lately that's been gold-rimmed porcelain bowls, Govino shatterproof glassware, jars of local Texas honey, and a few seasonal pieces stacked where you can't miss them. It's the part of the store that changes the most, so it's worth a look every time.

## The Jewelry Wall

Turn left and you hit the jewelry — and this is where people linger. We run a full slatwall of earrings and necklaces plus a locked gold case for the nicer pieces. **Julie Vos** anchors the case (the 24K-gold-plate stuff Texans keep choosing), and the **Rain** spinner holds the more playful, colorful earrings. There's a mirror right there for a reason: try things on.

## The Candle & Home Fragrance Shelf

Deeper in is the home fragrance section — **Voluspa** and **Greenleaf** candles, reed diffusers, room sprays, and the little Greenleaf sachets that make a $3 add-on to any gift. Pop a lid, take a sniff, and pick the scent that actually smells like the person you're buying for.

## The Card Spinners & Stationery Corner

We take cards seriously. Between the **Cardthartic** spinners, the **Freshcut** pop-up paper flowers, and the everyday birthday and thank-you walls, there's a card for the exact thing you're trying to say. Right next to it: colorful fountain pens, **Kaweco** writing tools, and notebooks for the people on your list who still love good paper.

## The Fun Stuff

Toward the back you'll find the parts that make kids (and a lot of adults) light up — the **TY** plush tower, **Swig** tumblers and water bottles, **Meri Meri** party goods, sunglasses, and a wall of gift bags so you can walk out wrapped and ready.

## Plan a Visit

The best way to understand Gugu Gifts is to walk it. Everything here was chosen by hand — not by an algorithm — and we're happy to help you build the perfect gift while you're in.
