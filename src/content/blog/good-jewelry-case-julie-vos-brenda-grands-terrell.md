## Why October, Not December

Both lines come in small quantities, and this is the part people learn the hard way: **when a piece sells, it usually doesn't come back that season.** What's in the case in October is largely what there is.

By mid-December the case is picked over — the popular bangles and the initial pieces go first, and the initials go in an unpredictable order depending on which letters people need. If there's a specific person and a specific piece in your head, October is when both still exist.

We'll hold a piece with a note. That costs you nothing and removes the December problem entirely.
