## For the People Who Still Love Paper

There's a certain kind of person who reads "stationery" and feels something. If that's you — or you're shopping for someone like that — the paper-goods corner at **Gugu Gifts** is the part of the store you'll want to budget extra time for.

Here's what lives there.

## The Cards

We carry cards the way a good bookstore carries books — broadly, and with taste.

- **Cardthartic** — the mini-cards-with-mighty-messages spinners, plus a full birthday and everyday wall. The photography-and-a-line format that says a lot in a little space.
- **Freshcut Paper** — the pop-up paper bouquets that open into a 3D arrangement. They're technically a card and emotionally a gift; people gasp.
- The everyday walls: birthday, thank-you, sympathy, congratulations, new baby — the cards you wish you'd already had in a drawer.

A card turns any small gift into "they thought about this." Never skip the card.

## The Pens

The pens are where the corner gets dangerous (in a good way). We stock colorful fountain pens in candy shades and the clean, German-made **Kaweco** writing tools that fans seek out specifically. A nice pen is one of the great under-$40 gifts — it feels personal, it lasts, and it doesn't require knowing someone's size or taste in anything.

## The Notebooks & Planners

For the list-makers and the journal-keepers:

- Bright **Kreatives Verhalten** creative pads and journals
- Daily planners, weekly planners, and to-do pads for the "I just need to get organized" crowd
- Teacher-leaning pieces — the "You Got This" and "The World Needs More Teachers Like You" notebooks that make easy end-of-year or thank-you gifts

## How We'd Build a Stationery Gift

- **Fountain pen + a journal** = a complete "new chapter" gift for a graduate or new job.
- **Freshcut pop-up bouquet + a Voluspa candle** = a get-well or thinking-of-you that ships flat and lands big.
- **A stack of Cardthartic cards tied with ribbon** = the gift for the friend who always has the right card ready (now they will).

## Come Browse It in Person

Paper is a touch-it-to-get-it category. Come run your hand over the covers, test a pen, and find the card that says the thing.
