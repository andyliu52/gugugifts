## The Voluspa Aisle Is Beautiful and Also Overwhelming

You've seen it. Walk into any well-curated gift shop and there's a wall of Voluspa: embossed tins, painted glass jars, scents you've never heard of next to scents you've smelled at every brunch in the last three years. Voluspa is one of the most-recognized luxury candle brands in the country for a reason — but if you're buying it as a gift, the choice paralysis is real.

This is our practical pairing guide. Built from what we actually carry on the shelves at **Gugu Gifts, 301 Tanger Dr, Suite 112, Terrell, TX 75160**, and what we've watched customers gravitate toward.

## How Voluspa Pricing Works (Quickly)

The most common gift size is the **5.5 oz** classic candle around **$22**. There are smaller travel tins, larger 9 oz jars, and the iconic large 18 oz vessels that double as decor pieces — but the 5.5 oz is where 80% of gifts land. It's the sweet spot of "feels real" and "doesn't feel showy."

## Pick By Personality

### "She has impeccable taste, kind of intimidates me"
**Lychee** (currently on our shelves at $22). Bright, slightly floral, slightly fruity, completely sophisticated. Universal upgrade from any grocery-store candle.

### "She loves fall, October is her whole brand"
Look for warm-spice scents in the **Japonica collection** (Baltic Amber, Goji Tarocco Orange variants). Voluspa rotates seasonal warmth scents — ask us what's currently in stock when you walk in.

### "She has kids and the house is chaos"
Avoid heavy florals or sweet bakery scents around kids — they read overwhelming in busy spaces. Cleaner citrus or fresh-air scents do better. Lychee works here too.

### "She's hosting a dinner party next week"
**Goji Tarocco Orange** or any citrus-forward Voluspa is the dinner-party move. Sophisticated, doesn't compete with the food, fills a dining room without dominating it.

## Pick By Room

| Room | Look for |
|------|----------|
| Living room | Mid-weight, sophisticated florals or amber — warm, welcoming |
| Bedroom | Lychee, Champagne Rose, anything subtle — overpowering scents in sleep spaces aren't great |
| Bathroom | Fresh florals, citrus — they fight humidity well |
| Kitchen | Avoid Voluspa here entirely. Clean kitchen scents are better as Greenleaf or Birch & Bloom; save Voluspa for living spaces |
| Office | Baltic Amber, woodsy notes — they hold up under HVAC |

## Voluspa Versus the Other Brands We Carry

Honest take from our own buying:

- **Voluspa** — best for *gifting*. The packaging is half the gift; she'll keep the jar long after the wax is gone.
- **Greenleaf** — best for *daily home use*. The flower diffusers ($34) outlast candles by months.
- **Birch & Bloom** — best for *under-$20 gifts and travel-friendly options*.

If you're shopping for someone who lights candles every day, Greenleaf and Birch & Bloom are arguably better daily-driver choices. But for *the gift moment* — the hand-it-over, watch-her-open-it moment — Voluspa wins.

## What to Pair It With

A Voluspa candle on its own is a complete gift. If you want to elevate it:

- **Add a Greenleaf Sachet ($3)** — drawer scent extender, costs almost nothing, looks generous
- **Add a hand-letterpress card** (we keep them at the register)
- **Skip the box**, use ribbon — Voluspa's jar is the visual
