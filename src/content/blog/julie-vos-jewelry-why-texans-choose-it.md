## The Brand You Keep Seeing on Wrists in This Part of Texas

If you've been to a wedding, a christening, or a brunch in Forney, Rockwall, or McKinney recently, you've probably seen **Julie Vos** — even if you didn't know the name. The 24-karat-gold-plate finish with the slightly textured surface, the little chain bracelets, the resin drop earrings in colors that match every event — it's the brand the women in this part of Texas keep choosing without making it a thing.

We carry Julie Vos at **Gugu Gifts** because it does something rare: it makes a *real* gift at a *reachable* price. Most pieces in our case sit between $16 and $48. That's the magic zone.

## Why It Works as an Heirloom Gift

"Heirloom" sounds dramatic, but it's the right word. The pieces wear well over years — they're not throwaway costume jewelry, but they're also not the $400 fine-jewelry tier that requires a velvet box ceremony.

Three reasons it lands as a milestone gift:

### 1. The 24K-gold-plate finish ages well
Unlike cheaper costume jewelry that tarnishes within a few wears, Julie Vos's finish holds. We've had customers come back two years later with pieces that still look new — the women who *wear them every day*, not the ones who keep them in a box.

### 2. The styling is timeless, not trendy
The Bahamian collection, the Chalcedony, the Cypress — these are designs that don't read as "2024" or "2026." They sit in the same lane as Kendra Scott's quieter pieces, but typically at a slightly lower price.

### 3. The price hits right for "this matters"
A $24 piece feels too small for a milestone. A $200 piece feels too big for "happy birthday." A $20–$48 piece — wrapped well, with a card — hits the *exact* note for: high school graduation, college acceptance, first job, milestone birthday, "you became a mom this year," "thank you for being there."
